# Maintainers and Governance

## Current maintainer

- [Ethan-Schenberg](https://github.com/Ethan-Schenberg) — founder and primary maintainer; responsible for architecture, Motion IR, vendor integrations, releases, issue triage, and pull-request review.

## Decision process

- Routine fixes and compatible features are reviewed through pull requests.
- Breaking Motion IR changes require a public issue, migration plan, and architecture decision record.
- Vendor compatibility claims require pinned upstream evidence and a reproducible test.
- Physical-robot paths require an explicit safety review separate from simulation acceptance.
- Licensing or provenance uncertainty blocks redistribution of the affected artifact, not development of the adapter itself.

As the contributor base grows, maintainership will be granted based on sustained review, issue triage, releases, documentation, and responsibility for a defined subsystem.
