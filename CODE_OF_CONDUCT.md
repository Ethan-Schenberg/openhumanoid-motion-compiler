# Code of Conduct

OHMC contributors are expected to keep technical discussion respectful, specific, and evidence-based.

Welcome questions from learners, distinguish verified behavior from assumptions, and treat safety concerns as engineering inputs rather than personal criticism. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate deception, and knowingly unsafe instructions are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or users. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers should explain moderation decisions when doing so would not compromise privacy or safety.
